# Why Online Orders Arrive in Big Boxes

**System:** Standardization of packaging to optimize warehouse logistics
**Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female voice (e.g., AvaNeural)
**Palette:** Dark theme (black canvas, white line art). Accent colors: **warm amber** (your item, you), **coral red** (waste, inefficiency, naive assumptions), **signal blue** (the system, standardization).
**Hero Element:** A small **warm amber** item (e.g., a tiny square/circle) that persists and is eventually contained by boxes.

**Continuity map:**
- **Scene 1 to 2:** The **warm amber** item transitions from being desired to being received in a large box.
- **Scene 2 to 3:** The large box remains, and its empty space is highlighted with **coral red** to show inefficiency.
- **Scene 3 to 4:** The concept of waste gives way to the **signal blue** system of standardized boxes, with the item placed inside.
- **Scene 4 to 5:** The standardized boxes become part of a larger, fast-moving **signal blue** logistics system, with **coral red** hints of the trade-off.
- **Scene 5 to 6:** The logistics system fades, returning to the stick figure receiving a box, now with understanding. The **warm amber** item is implied to be inside the box.
