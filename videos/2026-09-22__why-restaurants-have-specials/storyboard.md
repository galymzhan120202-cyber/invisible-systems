# Why Restaurants Offer 'Daily Specials'

*   **System:** Inventory management and demand smoothing incentives.
*   **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult female voice (e.g., AvaNeural).
*   **Palette:** Dark canvas (black), white line art. Accent colors: `signal blue` (the system/mechanism), `warm amber` (your desire/the special dish), `coral red` (naive assumption).
*   **Hero Element:** A `plate` or `dish` that morphs into abstract representations of ingredients and menu choices.
