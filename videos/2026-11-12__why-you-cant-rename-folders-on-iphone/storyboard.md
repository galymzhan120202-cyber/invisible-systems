# Production Plan: Why you can't rename iPhone folders

**System:** Opinionated design / user segmentation
**Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult female (e.g., AvaNeural)
**Palette:** Signal blue (system/rules), Warm amber (user desire/customization), Coral red (frustration/limitations)
**Hero Element:** An iPhone screen, with app icons and a folder icon, which transforms to reveal system design.

**Continuity map:**
- **Scene 1 to 2:** From a wide shot of the iPhone screen and user interaction to a close-up on the folder name input.
- **Scene 2 to 3:** From the frustrating input box to the user's wider reaction and search for answers, still implying the iPhone context.
- **Scene 3 to 4:** From the user's confusion to the iPhone screen transforming to reveal the underlying design principle.
- **Scene 4 to 5:** From the abstract design blueprint to a visual metaphor of choices and consequences.
- **Scene 5 to 6:** From the consequence of the design choice back to the user's perspective, now seeing the iPhone screen with a new understanding of its "invisible rules."
