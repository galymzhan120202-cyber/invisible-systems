# Why Supermarket Carts Are So Big

- **System:** The perceived emptiness of a large cart encourages shoppers to fill it, leading to larger purchases.
- **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult female (e.g., AvaNeural).
- **Palette:** Warm amber (YOU/desire), signal blue (the system/mechanism), coral red (naive model/internal urge).
- **Hero Element:** The stick-figure shopper and their shopping cart.

- **Scene 3 to 4:** The cart transforms from a simple object of contemplation into an active 'system' element, highlighted by signal blue and subtle movement cues.
- **Scene 4 to 5:** The abstract system's influence is concretized as the stick figure acts on the psychological prompt, filling the cart with items.
