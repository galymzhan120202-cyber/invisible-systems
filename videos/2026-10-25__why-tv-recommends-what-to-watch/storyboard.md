# Why Your TV Knows What You Want to Watch

*   **System:** Recommendation algorithms based on viewing history and collaborative filtering.
*   **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female voice.
*   **Palette:** Black canvas, white line art. Accent colors: signal blue (algorithm/system), warm amber (viewer/desire), coral red (naive/wrong assumption).
*   **Hero Element:** An amber stick figure (the viewer) and a black screen/content grid that visually represents data and connections.

**Continuity map:**
The amber stick figure (YOU) and the black TV screen are consistent across all scenes. The content icons (abstract white shapes) appear and are manipulated by the system. The blue "brain" icon and connecting lines represent the invisible algorithm, while coral red represents the viewer's initial naive assumptions.
