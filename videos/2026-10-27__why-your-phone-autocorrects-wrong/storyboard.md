# Storyboard: Why Your Phone Autocorrects Wrong

**System:** Predictive text models prioritizing common errors
**Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult female. Natural American English.
**Palette:** Signal blue (system/rule), Warm amber (user/desire), Coral red (naive error model)
**Hero Element:** A single line of text that starts as amber, gets tangled in coral red, and is then corrected by signal blue.

- Scene 1 to 2: Phone screen zooms out then back in, phone screen shakes.
- Scene 2 to 3: Wider shot of confused figure.
- Scene 3 to 4: Screen morphs and transforms as data floods in.
- Scene 4 to 5: Individual dot struggles against the collective.
- Scene 5 to 6: Zoom out, figure confident, screen glows blue.
