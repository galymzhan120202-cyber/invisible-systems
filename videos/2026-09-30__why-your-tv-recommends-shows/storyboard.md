# Why Your TV Recommends What to Watch

**System:** Collaborative filtering algorithms and user data

**Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female voice (en-US-AvaNeural or en-US-JennyNeural)

**Palette:**
- Warm amber: User's desires, preferences, "you"
- Signal blue: The invisible system, network connections, algorithms
- Coral red: Naive assumptions, false understanding

**Hero Element:** A simple stick figure (the user, amber) and a rectangular screen/panel.

**Continuity map:**
The amber stick figure (YOU) remains a consistent hero element. The rectangular screen/panel evolves and transforms across all scenes:
- Scene 1 to 2: The blank screen becomes a recommendation grid.
- Scene 2 to 3: The recommendation grid fades, replaced by a naive model (brain/tiny curator) on screen.
- Scene 3 to 4: The naive model dissolves, replaced by the signal blue collaborative filtering network.
- Scene 4 to 5: The network consolidates into a bounded cluster.
- Scene 5 to 6: The cluster expands into an infinite, all-encompassing network, filling the entire frame.
