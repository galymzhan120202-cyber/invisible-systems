## Storyboard: Why Supermarkets Put Popular Items At The End

*   **System:** Layout design to expose shoppers to more products
*   **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female voice
*   **Palette:** Dark theme (black canvas, white line art), warm amber (you/desire), signal blue (system/path), coral red (naive/confusion)
*   **Hero Element:** Stick figure with shopping cart (warm amber outline), supermarket aisles/layout

**Continuity map:**
*   **Stick figure with shopping cart:** Persists across all scenes, always with a warm amber outline on the cart.
*   **Supermarket layout/path:** Starts as simple lines, becomes a confusing maze, then a clearly defined signal blue path from an overhead view.
*   **Milk carton icon:** Present as the distant goal, then as the destination at the end of the signal blue path.
