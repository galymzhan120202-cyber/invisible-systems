# Why the Fastest App Loads First

- **System:** Prioritization in mobile operating systems based on performance metrics
- **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult female
- **Palette:** Black canvas, white line art. Accent colors: `signal blue` (system/rules), `warm amber` (user/desire), `coral red` (naive/wrong model).
- **Hero Element:** A stick figure user (amber), a generic smartphone (white outline), and simple, abstract app icons (white outlines, sometimes filled with accent colors). The phone's internal OS is represented by a pulsing signal blue core.
