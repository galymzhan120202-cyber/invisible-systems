# Production Plan: Why Your Phone Battery Drains So Fast

**System:** Background processes and connectivity demands on mobile devices
**Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult female (en-US-AvaNeural)
**Accent Palette:** warm amber (user's desire/phone state), coral red (naive actions/depletion), signal blue (invisible system/processes)
**Hero Element:** A smartphone, its battery bar, and internal network activity.

**Continuity map:**
- **Scene 1-2:** The phone transitions from being held to being in a pocket, with the battery bar consistently visible and draining.
- **Scene 2-3:** The phone is taken out of the pocket, and the battery bar continues to reflect the ongoing drain, leading to the stick figure's confusion.
- **Scene 3-4:** The phone remains present, but the camera zooms into its internal structure, revealing the hidden blue network that explains the drain.
- **Scene 4-5:** The internal view of the phone persists, with the blue network's activity intensifying and the battery bar's drain accelerating.
- **Scene 5-6:** The camera pulls back to the stick figure holding the phone, which now subtly displays the understood blue network, showing a full circle of understanding.
