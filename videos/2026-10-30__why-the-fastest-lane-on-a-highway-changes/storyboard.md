# Why The Fastest Lane On A Highway Changes

*   **System:** Dynamic traffic flow and emergent congestion patterns.
*   **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female voice.
*   **Palette:** `warm amber` (viewer's car), `coral red` (frustration/slowdown), `signal blue` (traffic waves/system).
*   **Hero Element:** A single `warm amber` stick-figure car, highway lanes, and small white traffic dots.

**Continuity map:**
The highway lines persist across all scenes. The `warm amber` car is a persistent hero element, moving and reacting. The white traffic dots are always present, their behavior evolving from individual movement to exhibiting `signal blue` wave patterns. The camera generally tracks the `warm amber` car, but pulls back to show the system.
