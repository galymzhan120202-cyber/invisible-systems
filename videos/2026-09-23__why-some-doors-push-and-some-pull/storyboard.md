# Production Plan: Why Doors Open Differently

*   **System:** Design conventions for crowd management and safety
*   **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult female voice (e.g., AvaNeural, JennyNeural)
*   **Palette:** Warm amber (for 'YOU'), coral red (for confusion/naive model), signal blue (for revealed system)
*   **Hero Element:** A simple door frame (white) that evolves with different opening mechanisms and contextual elements.

| # | Time | Story beat | Stick-figure scene | Motion / camera / transition | English VO | SFX |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–10s | **YOU + NEED:** Expecting easy passage | A stick figure (warm amber) walks confidently towards a plain rectangular door frame (white). | Camera tracks amber stick figure moving left-to-right. Figure pauses before door. | Every day, countless doors stand between you and where you need to be. You approach, expecting a simple, intuitive move to open your path quickly. | `focus.mp3` (0.6s) as figure stops; `whoosh-fast.mp3` (2.8s) as they gesture to open. |
| 2 | 10–20s | **GO:** Unexpected resistance / confusion | The amber stick figure attempts to open the door, but struggles. The door has a small push plate on one side and a handle on the other, but the figure tries the wrong action. A small coral red "X" appears briefly over their head. | Stick figure pushes against the pull-handle door, then pulls against the push-plate door. Frustrated, jerky movements. | But then it happens: you push a door that pulls, or pull one that pushes. A moment of awkward fumbling, and suddenly you feel silly. | `drop-thud.mp3` (10.5s) on first failed attempt; `click-soft.mp3` (12.8s) on second (successful but fumbled) attempt; `stack-collapse.mp3` (16.0s) as they feel silly. |
| 3 | 20–30s | **SEARCH:** Blaming bad design | The amber stick figure stands confused in front of the door (which now has both a push plate and a pull handle visible, conflicting). A large, blurry coral red question mark hovers over the door. | Camera slightly zooms in on the door and the confused figure. Coral red question mark pulses softly. | Why this constant confusion? You blame bad design or random inconsistency. It feels like doors are deliberately trying to trick you, making tasks frustrating. | `whoosh.mp3` (20.8s) as question mark appears; `stack-collapse.mp3` (24.5s) as confusion deepens. |
| 4 | 30–40s | **FIND:** The real reason - safety design | The door transforms into a wide, signal blue emergency exit door with a horizontal push bar. Multiple anonymous white stick figures smoothly flow outwards through it. | The coral red question mark dissolves. The door morphs from confusing white to clear signal blue. Camera follows the outward flow of white stick figures. | The truth is, many doors are designed for crowds and emergencies, not individual convenience. Fire exits must push OUT easily, without obstruction, for safety. | `transform.mp3` (30.5s) as door changes; `focus.mp3` (34.0s) on the push bar. |
| 5 | 40–50s | **TAKE:** The cost of collective safety | Close-up on the signal blue emergency door's push bar. A tiny, frustrated coral red stick figure struggles on one side, while a stream of white stick figures glides effortlessly through the other. | Camera pans slowly across the push bar. The coral red figure is small in the corner, contrasting with the smooth flow. | So, your occasional awkward push-pull is the silent cost of a system prioritizing collective safety. Your momentary confusion ensures everyone can exit swiftly. | `complete-done.mp3` (40.8s) for the realization; `click-soft.mp3` (44.5s) as the system quietly works. |
| 6 | 50–60s | **RETURN + CHANGE:** Informed action | The amber stick figure returns to a door frame (white) which clearly displays a signal blue push plate. The figure confidently pushes it open and walks through. | Camera tracks amber stick figure. A clear signal blue push plate appears. Stick figure pushes smoothly. Door opens easily. | Now, you'll spot the subtle cues: a flat plate means push, a handle implies pull. You understand the system, making you an expert observer. | `focus.mp3` (50.5s) as figure observes door; `whoosh-fast.mp3` (52.8s) as door opens; `complete-done.mp3` (57.0s) for the new understanding. |

**Continuity map:**
- **Scene 1-2:** The amber stick figure and the basic door frame remain central, transitioning from expectation to confusion.
- **Scene 2-3:** The door's conflicting elements (push/pull) persist, leading to the coral red confusion state of the stick figure.
- **Scene 3-4:** The confusing door transforms into a clear, functional signal blue emergency exit, shifting focus from individual frustration to systemic purpose.
- **Scene 4-5:** The signal blue emergency door and the concept of crowd flow continue, with a visual contrast between individual struggle and collective ease.
- **Scene 5-6:** The amber stick figure returns to a one-on-one interaction with a door, but now applies the knowledge gained from the signal blue system.
