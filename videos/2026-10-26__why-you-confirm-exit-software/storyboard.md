# Why You Confirm You Want to Exit Software

*   **System:** Loss aversion design to prevent accidental data loss
*   **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female voice
*   **Palette:** `signal blue` (system/protection), `warm amber` (user's work/data), `coral red` (frustration/loss/naive model)
*   **Hero Element:** The computer screen, the document/data icon, and the pop-up window.
