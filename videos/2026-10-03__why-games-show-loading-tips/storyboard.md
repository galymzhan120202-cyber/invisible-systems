# Why Games Show Loading Screen Tips

*   **System:** Player engagement and cognitive load management during waits
*   **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult female voice (e.g., AvaNeural)
*   **Accent Palette:** Warm Amber (Player/Desire), Coral Red (Frustration/Boredom), Signal Blue (System/Tips)
*   **Hero Element:** An amber stick-figure player with a controller, and a screen that displays gameplay, then a loading bar, then tips, then gameplay again.

**Continuity map:**
The **amber stick-figure player** and the **game screen** persist throughout all six scenes. The screen transforms from showing active gameplay (Scene 1), to an empty loading bar (Scene 2), to a frustratingly slow loading bar (Scene 3), to a loading screen filled with engaging signal blue tips (Scenes 4-5), and finally back to enriched gameplay (Scene 6). The player's posture and subtle facial cues reflect their changing emotional state, from excitement to boredom, then re-engagement and informed understanding.
