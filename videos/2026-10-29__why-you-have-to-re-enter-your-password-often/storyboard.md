# Production Plan: Why You Have To Re-enter Your Password Often

*   **System:** Security protocols balancing convenience and risk through session management.
*   **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult female voice (e.g., AvaNeural).
*   **Palette:** `signal blue` (system/security), `warm amber` (user/convenience), `coral red` (frustration/risk).
*   **Hero Element:** The stick figure (YOU) and a 'lock' icon that transforms into a 'session token', then a 'timer', and finally a 'shield'.

**Continuity map:**
*   **Scene 1-2:** The screen transitions from a seamless app interface to an intrusive login prompt, reflecting the user's shift from flow to interruption.
*   **Scene 2-3:** The login prompt persists as the user's emotional state changes from surprise to frustrated resignation, indicated by their actions.
*   **Scene 3-4:** The frustrating login interface fades, revealing the underlying 'session token' mechanism, drawing the viewer's focus to the system itself.
*   **Scene 4-5:** The 'session token' (now a timer) remains central, and the visual metaphor expands to show the trade-off between user convenience and data security.
*   **Scene 5-6:** The implications of the system are resolved into a clear understanding, as the protective aspect is visualized with the final shield protecting the user.
