*   **System:** Reducing friction and leveraging intermittent rewards
*   **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female voice
*   **Palette:** Black canvas, white stick-figures, signal blue (system/rules), warm amber (user/desire), coral red (friction/cost)
*   **Hero Element:** A smartphone screen, interacting with a stick-figure user.
