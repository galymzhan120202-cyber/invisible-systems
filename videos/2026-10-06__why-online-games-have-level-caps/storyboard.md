# Production Plan: Why Online Games Have Level Caps

**System:** Player retention and social dynamics
**Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult female (e.g., AvaNeural)
**Palette:** Dark theme. Accent colors: `warm amber` (player/desire), `coral red` (naive view/obstacle), `signal blue` (the system/mechanism).
**Hero Elements:** An amber stick-figure player, and a "level indicator" that transforms from an XP bar into a level cap (initially coral red wall, then signal blue gate).

**Continuity map:**
- **Scene 1-2:** The amber stick-figure and the concept of "leveling up" continues, but the XP bar hits a hard stop.
- **Scene 2-3:** The coral-red level cap remains a central, unmoving obstacle.
- **Scene 3-4:** The coral-red wall transforms into the signal-blue gate, revealing a new perspective on the game world.
- **Scene 4-5:** The gate's function (pacing/moderation) is demonstrated by its effect on a powerful player, with the gate appearing as a regulatory element.
- **Scene 5-6:** The gate (now signal blue) is integrated into the background as the amber stick-figure, now enlightened, interacts with the game's social elements.
