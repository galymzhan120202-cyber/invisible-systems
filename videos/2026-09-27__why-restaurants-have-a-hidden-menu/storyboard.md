# Why Restaurants Have Secret Menus

*   **System:** Creating exclusivity and insider status through perceived hidden options.
*   **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female voice.
*   **Palette:** `warm amber` (viewer's desire, feeling of exclusivity/status), `coral red` (awkwardness, naive searching, illusion), `signal blue` (the revealed system/mechanism).
*   **Hero Element:** A menu card/icon that transforms.
