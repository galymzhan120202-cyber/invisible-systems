# Production Plan: Why App Notifications Feel So Urgent

*   **System:** Intermittent reinforcement and attention economy
*   **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult female voice (e.g., `en-US-AvaNeural`)
*   **Palette:** `signal blue` (system/rule), `warm amber` (YOU/desire), `coral red` (naive model)
*   **Hero Element:** Stick figure (YOU) and a transforming notification icon

| # | Time | Story beat | Stick-figure scene | Motion / camera / transition | English VO | SFX |
|---|------|------------|--------------------|------------------------------|------------|-----|
| 1 | 0–10s | **YOU + NEED** | A `warm amber` stick figure sits at a desk, surrounded by a pulsing `warm amber` focus aura. | Camera focuses on the stick figure, slight zoom-in on the aura. Aura pulses gently. | You’re deep in focus, absorbed in your task. Your mind is sharp, concentration unwavering. You just want to stay in that productive flow, undisturbed. | `focus.mp3` (0.6s), `whoosh.mp3` (4.0s) |
| 2 | 10–20s | **GO** | A small `signal blue` notification icon (e.g., a bell) appears in the corner, then grows slightly. The `warm amber` aura around the stick figure flickers and shrinks. The stick figure turns its head towards the icon. | Notification icon pops into existence and expands. Aura dissipates. Stick figure's head snaps to look at the icon. | Suddenly, a tiny flicker. A notification pops. You feel an irresistible tug. Focus breaks, you reach for your device. | `whoosh-fast.mp3` (10.8s), `click-soft.mp3` (14.5s) |
| 3 | 20–30s | **SEARCH** | The stick figure is now looking directly at the `signal blue` notification icon. A `coral red` thought bubble appears above its head, containing a question mark. | Camera holds on the stick figure and icon. Thought bubble with question mark appears and shimmers briefly. | Why does that alert feel urgent? You might assume every notification is crucial, a vital piece you can't miss. | `focus.mp3` (21.0s), `click-soft.mp3` (26.0s) |
| 4 | 30–40s | **FIND** | The `coral red` thought bubble dissolves. The `signal blue` notification icon morphs into a simplified `signal blue` slot machine lever. Small, trivial `signal blue` dots and occasional larger, brighter `signal blue` star shapes fall from it. | Thought bubble dissolves. Notification icon transforms. Dots and stars fall intermittently from the lever. | But the real mechanism is intermittent reinforcement. Most notifications are trivial, yet some deliver a true reward. That unpredictability trains you to check every time. | `transform.mp3` (31.0s), `complete-done.mp3` (37.0s) |
| 5 | 40–50s | **TAKE** | The `signal blue` slot machine lever continues to drop dots and stars. The `warm amber` stick figure's body appears stretched thin, with fragmented `warm amber` lines radiating outwards, representing scattered attention. | Stick figure visually fragments and distorts. Falling shapes from the lever continue. | This system has a cost. Your attention fragments, constantly pulled. You pay with lost focus and anxiety, always waiting for that next hit. | `stack-collapse.mp3` (42.0s), `drop-thud.mp3` (47.0s) |
| 6 | 50–60s | **RETURN + CHANGE** | The `warm amber` stick figure is restored, now looking calmly at a smaller, neutral `signal blue` notification icon. The background shows faint, intermittent `signal blue` flashes. The stick figure then turns away, now aware. | Stick figure restores, posture shifts from anxious to calm. Faint flashes appear in background. Stick figure turns away from the icon. | Tomorrow, the pings will still come. But now you see the hidden machinery: apps aren't just sending messages; they're training you, hooked on the *chance* of reward. | `whoosh-fast.mp3` (51.0s), `transform.mp3` (55.0s), `complete-done.mp3` (58.0s) |

**Continuity map:**
*   **Scene 1 → 2:** The `warm amber` stick figure remains, its focus `aura` dissipates as the `signal blue` notification icon appears and draws its attention.
*   **Scene 2 → 3:** The `warm amber` stick figure and `signal blue` notification icon remain static, the scene shifts to the stick figure's internal `coral red` thought process.
*   **Scene 3 → 4:** The `coral red` thought bubble dissolves, and the `signal blue` notification icon `transforms` into the `signal blue` slot machine lever, revealing the system.
*   **Scene 4 → 5:** The `warm amber` stick figure and `signal blue` lever remain, but the stick figure's form distorts to show the `cost` of fragmented attention.
*   **Scene 5 → 6:** The `warm amber` stick figure restores its form, now observing the `signal blue` notification icon with new understanding, as faint `signal blue` flashes hint at future pings.
