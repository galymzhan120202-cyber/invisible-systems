# Why The Cheapest Wine Isn't Always The Best Deal

*   **System:** Menu engineering and anchoring bias in pricing
*   **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female (e.g., AvaNeural)
*   **Palette:** signal blue (system/rules), warm amber (customer/desire), coral red (naive model/trap)
*   **Hero Element:** A stick-figure customer (warm amber) interacting with a wine list/bottle. The wine list itself will transform to reveal the system.
