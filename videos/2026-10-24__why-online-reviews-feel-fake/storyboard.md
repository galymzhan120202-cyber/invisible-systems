# Why So Many Online Reviews Feel Fake

*   **System:** Incentive structures for generating and filtering user-generated content
*   **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult female (e.g., AvaNeural)
*   **Palette:** `signal blue` (system/filter), `warm amber` (YOU/positive review), `coral red` (fake/negative review/suspicion)
*   **Hero Element:** A `Product Box` (simple rectangle) and `Stars` (representing reviews)
