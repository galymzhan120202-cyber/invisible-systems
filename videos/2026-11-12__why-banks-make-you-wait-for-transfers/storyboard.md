# Why Banks Make You Wait for Transfers

*   **System:** Settlement cycles / risk management
*   **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female voice (natural American English)
*   **Palette:** Black canvas, white stick figures. Accent colors: warm amber (user/money), coral red (naive/wrong idea), signal blue (system/rules).
*   **Hero Element:** An amber "money block" or digital token that represents the transfer, moving or being held.

**Continuity map:**
