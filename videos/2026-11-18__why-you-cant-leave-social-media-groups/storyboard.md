# Why It's Hard To Leave Some Online Groups

- **System:** network effects and social pressure within digital communities
- **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult female voice (e.g., AvaNeural)
- **Palette:** Black canvas, white line art, signal blue (system), warm amber (YOU/desire), coral red (friction/naive model)
- **Hero Element:** An amber stick figure (YOU) connected by lines (social ties/notifications) to a central blue group icon or a web of smaller group nodes. The connections persist and evolve.
