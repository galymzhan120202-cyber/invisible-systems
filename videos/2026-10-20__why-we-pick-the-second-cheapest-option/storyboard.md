# Why We Pick the Second Cheapest Option

*   **System:** Anchoring and perceived value in pricing strategy
*   **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female voice (e.g., AvaNeural)
*   **Palette:** Dark theme (black canvas, white line art). Accent colors: **signal blue** (system/anchor), **warm amber** (viewer/chosen option), **coral red** (naive model/dismissed option).
*   **Hero Element:** A warm amber stick figure (the viewer) and three vertical bars representing product options/prices.

**Continuity map:**
*   **Stick figure:** Appears in Scene 1, persists throughout, its actions and expressions change with understanding.
*   **Product/Price Options:** Represented by three vertical bars/icons. Their state (empty outlines, filled, dismissed, re-contextualized as an anchor) changes across scenes. The medium bar (the target of the system) is consistently present.
*   **Colors:** Warm amber for the stick figure, coral red for dismissed/naive perspective, signal blue for the system/anchor. These colors highlight different elements across scenes.
