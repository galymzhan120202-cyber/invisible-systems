# Why Software Subscriptions Auto-Renew

**System:** Default options and opt-out friction
**Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female voice (e.g., AvaNeural)
**Palette:** warm amber (user/desire), signal blue (system/default), coral red (friction/naive model)
**Hero Element:** The stick figure (user, amber) and a "subscription" concept represented by a screen interface and transforming icons (timer, dollar stack, toggle switch).

**Continuity map:**
*   **The User:** The amber stick figure is the persistent protagonist, experiencing the subscription process and evolving in understanding.
*   **The Interface:** The screen at the desk represents the software interface, through which the user interacts with the system.
*   **The Subscription Concept:** This is represented by transforming icons: the "1 Month Trial" badge, the spinning clock, the stacking dollar bills, and finally the "Auto-Renew" toggle switch. These icons link the initial action to its consequences and the underlying mechanism.
