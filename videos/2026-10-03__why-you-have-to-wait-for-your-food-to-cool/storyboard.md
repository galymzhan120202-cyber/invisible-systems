# Why You Have to Wait for Food to Cool

- **System:** Thermodynamic principles and food safety considerations
- **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female voice (en-US-AvaNeural or en-US-JennyNeural)
- **Palette:** Dark theme (black canvas, white line art). Accent colors: **warm amber** (food, desire), **coral red** (danger zone, naive assumption), **signal blue** (safe cooling, the revealed mechanism).
- **Hero Element:** A stick figure and a bowl of steaming food. The food's steam and internal representation will change to indicate temperature and danger.
