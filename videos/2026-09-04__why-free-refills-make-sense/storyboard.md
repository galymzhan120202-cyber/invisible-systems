# Why free refills make business sense

*   **System:** Marginal cost near zero / Anchoring perceived value
*   **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult female (e.g., AvaNeural)
*   **Accent Palette:** Warm amber (customer's desire/value), Signal blue (the business system/mechanism), Coral red (the naive assumption/cost)
*   **Hero Element:** A stick figure holding an amber drink cup, and a drink dispenser/fountain.
