# Production Plan: Why You Can Only Buy Three Items on Sale

**System:** Purchase limits as a price discrimination and inventory management tool
**Narrator:** Energetic adult female (AvaNeural/JennyNeural)
**Palette:** `warm amber` (shopper/desire), `coral red` (naive assumptions/restriction), `signal blue` (the revealed system/mechanism)
**Hero Element:** A shopping cart, initially simple, then transforming to represent different customer segments and the system itself.

**Continuity map:**
- **Scene 1 to 2:** The amber shopper and the shopping cart persist. The environment (supermarket aisle) remains consistent.
- **Scene 2 to 3:** The shopping cart with items remains. The "LIMIT 3" sign is still present, moving to the background.
- **Scene 3 to 4:** The concept of the shopping cart remains, but it transforms to illustrate the two customer segments.
- **Scene 4 to 5:** The distinction between amber (bargain hunter) and blue (full-price customer) is maintained, with the store icon as a central element.
- **Scene 5 to 6:** The amber shopper and the "LIMIT 3" sign return, but the shopper's perception of the sign (represented by the blue aura) is changed. The shopping cart integrates the blue system lines.
