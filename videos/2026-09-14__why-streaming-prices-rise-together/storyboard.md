# Production Plan: Why streaming services keep raising prices together

**System:** Tacit coordination / price umbrella without collusion
**Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult female (e.g., AvaNeural)
**Palette:** Dark theme (black canvas, white line art). Accent colors: **signal blue** (system/rules), **warm amber** (YOU/desire), **coral red** (naive/wrong model).
**Hero Elements:** A stick figure (YOU), simple streaming service icons (N, D, H, P), and a "price tag" or "money stack" concept.

**Continuity map:**
*   **Scene 1 → 2:** Streaming icons persist; their prices change. Stick figure (YOU) persists, changes expression.
*   **Scene 2 → 3:** Stick figure transforms into a detective. Streaming icons persist, change arrangement (huddled) and show wrong model (red lines).
*   **Scene 3 → 4:** Stick figure transforms back to regular YOU. Streaming icons separate and show observation/follow mechanism (blue elements).
*   **Scene 4 → 5:** Streaming icons persist, all show higher prices, blue umbrella appears. Stick figure looks at their wallet.
*   **Scene 5 → 6:** Streaming icons persist, umbrella persists. Stick figure is back on couch, gains understanding (lightbulb).
