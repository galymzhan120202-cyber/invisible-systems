# Why Games Block Your Progress

- **System:** Progression pacing and monetization loops in online games.
- **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female voice.
- **Palette:** Signal blue for the system/gate, warm amber for the player, white for the path, coral red for frustration/grind.
- **Hero Element:** An amber stick-figure player, and a winding white path that gets blocked by a blue gate.
