# Production Plan: Why You Get a Different Cashier Each Time

**System:** Staff scheduling and labor optimization in retail
**Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female voice (en-US-AvaNeural)
**Palette:** Signal blue (system/rules), Warm amber (viewer/desire), Coral red (naive model). White for general elements.
**Hero Element:** A simple clock icon, subtly present in most scenes, animating to reflect time/scheduling. The "checkout counter" is also a persistent element.

**Continuity map:**
- **Scene 1 to 2:** Amber customer and white checkout counter persist, but customer moves to a new counter with a different cashier. Clock icon persists.
- **Scene 2 to 3:** Amber customer persists, now pondering. Checkout counter and cashier are implied but not central. Clock icon persists.
- **Scene 3 to 4:** Focus shifts entirely to the clock icon, which transforms.
- **Scene 4 to 5:** Returns to a checkout counter context, with the invisible system (blue grid) now visible. Amber customer and a new cashier.
- **Scene 5 to 6:** Amber customer persists, walking away, with the clock icon now subtly glowing.
