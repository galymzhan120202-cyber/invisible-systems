# Why Your Inbox Sorts Emails Into Folders

*   **System:** Algorithmic categorization and user preference nudging
*   **Narrator:** Energetic adult female (AvaNeural or JennyNeural)
*   **Palette:** Dark theme (black canvas, white line art). Accent colors: **signal blue** (system/rules), **warm amber** (you/desire), **coral red** (naive/wrong model).
*   **Hero Element:** An 'inbox' rectangle that initially fills with disparate 'email' lines/shapes, then morphs into a segmented box with distinct folders, and finally becomes a transparent overlay. The stick figure 'You' interacting with it.
