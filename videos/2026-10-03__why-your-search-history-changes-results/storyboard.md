**Continuity map:** The amber stick figure, the search bar, and the concept of search results (represented by blocks/cards) are persistent elements. The search bar's outline shifts to signal blue to represent the revealed system, and the result blocks change color (white -> coral red -> blue-influenced white) and arrangement (grid -> cluster -> bubble -> shattered) to reflect the user's journey from naive expectation to informed awareness.
