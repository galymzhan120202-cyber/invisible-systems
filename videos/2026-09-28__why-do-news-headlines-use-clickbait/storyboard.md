# Why News Headlines Are So Clickbaity

*   **System:** Attention economics and engagement metrics
*   **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female voice (e.g., AvaNeural)
*   **Palette:** `warm amber` (YOU / desire for information), `signal blue` (algorithms / system), `coral red` (naive assumptions / frustration)
*   **Hero Element:** A `rectangle` representing a "headline tile" or "screen content". It will be the focus, transforming and reacting.

**Continuity map:**
The `warm amber` stick figure (YOU) is present in all scenes, evolving from hopeful to frustrated to understanding. The `rectangle` representing a headline/screen content is the persistent hero element, transforming from simple, to distorted, to algorithmic, to fragmented, and finally to a known entity. The vertical scrolling motion of the background is consistent.
