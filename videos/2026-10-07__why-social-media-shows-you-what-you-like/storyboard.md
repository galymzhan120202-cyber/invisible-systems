# Why Social Media Shows You What You Like

- **System**: Engagement loops and algorithmic curation
- **Narrator**: Bright, energetic adult-female voice
- **Palette**: Signal blue (algorithm/system), Warm amber (You/your desires), Coral red (the 'bubble' or naive perception)
- **Hero Element**: A stick figure (YOU, warm amber) and a vertical 'feed' of content blocks.
