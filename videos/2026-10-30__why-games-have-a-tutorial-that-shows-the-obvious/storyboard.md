# Why Games Have Tutorials For Obvious Actions

*   **System:** Player onboarding and error prevention for new users
*   **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female voice
*   **Palette:** Signal blue (system/guidance), warm amber (experienced player/desire), coral red (frustration/error)
*   **Hero Element:** An 'experienced player' stick figure (amber) and a 'game screen' that transforms, interacting with a 'new player' stick figure (blue).

*   **Scene 2 -> 3:** The amber player observes as the concept of the "new player" (blue stick figure) and "developer" perspective (blue stick figure) are introduced.

*   **Scene 4 -> 5:** The amber player observes the new player's successful engagement, and the visualization shifts to compare the small "cost" (coral red) of tutorials with the large "benefit" of a broad player base (signal blue).
*   **Scene 5 -> 6:** Both the amber and blue players are now seen as part of a cohesive game world, with the amber player gaining a new, blue-hued understanding, signifying changed perspective.
