# Why Supermarket Music Makes You Spend More

*   **System:** Tempo manipulation of background music to influence shopping pace
*   **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult female (e.g., AvaNeural)
*   **Palette:** Black canvas, white line art. Accent colors: signal blue (system/music), warm amber (shopper), coral red (naive assumption).
*   **Hero Element:** A stick figure shopper (warm amber) and a wavy line representing music (initially white, then signal blue).
