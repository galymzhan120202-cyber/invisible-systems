# Production Plan: Why boarding a plane is slower than it should be

**System:** Queueing / parallelism lost to aisle and luggage conflicts
**Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult female (en-US-AvaNeural)
**Palette:** Dark theme (black canvas, white line art) with accent colors: signal blue (system/rules), warm amber (YOU/desire), coral red (friction/naive model).
**Hero Elements:** An amber stick figure (YOU), a white abstract plane outline that becomes translucent to reveal interior, and simple white luggage blocks.

**Continuity map:**
- **Scene 1 → Scene 2:** The amber stick figure transitions from standing at the gate to joining a boarding line.
- **Scene 2 → Scene 3:** The amber stick figure moves from the external line into the internal, congested plane aisle.
- **Scene 3 → Scene 4:** The perspective shifts from a close-up of congestion inside the plane to a wider, transparent view of the entire plane's tube structure.
- **Scene 4 → Scene 5:** The camera zooms back into the aisle, focusing on the individual actions (stowing luggage) that create systemic bottlenecks.
- **Scene 5 → Scene 6:** The amber stick figure moves from the bottlenecked aisle to a seated position, and the scene widens to show the plane interior with an emphasis on the newly understood aisle mechanism.
