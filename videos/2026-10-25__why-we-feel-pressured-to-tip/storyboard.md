# Why You Feel Pressured to Tip More

*   **System:** Social cues and menu design influencing tipping behavior
*   **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female voice (e.g., `en-US-AvaNeural`)
*   **Palette:** Black canvas, white line art. Accent colors: warm amber (the diner/you), signal blue (the system/tip options), coral red (naive assumption/pressure).
*   **Hero Element:** The Diner (warm amber stick figure) and The Bill (white rectangle with blue accents).
