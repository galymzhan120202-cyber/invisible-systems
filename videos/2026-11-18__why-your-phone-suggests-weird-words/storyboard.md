# Why Your Phone Suggests Weird Words

*   **System:** Predictive text algorithm biases based on training data.
*   **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female voice (e.g., AvaNeural).
*   **Palette:** Dark theme (black canvas, white line art). Accent colors: **signal blue** (the system/data flow), **warm amber** (the user's intent/input), **coral red** (bizarre suggestions/biases/naive assumptions).
*   **Hero Element:** A stick figure representing "YOU," and a smartphone. Abstract "word blocks" will represent text and data, morphing in color and shape.
