## Storyboard: Why Every Queue You Join Is The Slow One

*   **System:** Confirmation bias and selective memory recall in waiting lines.
*   **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult female voice (e.g., AvaNeural).
*   **Palette:** Dark canvas, white line art. Accent colors: warm amber (You/desire), coral red (naive model/frustration), signal blue (the system revealed).
*   **Hero Element:** The amber stick figure ("You") and the dynamic lines of white stick figures representing queues, along with abstract representations of time and memory.

**Continuity map:** The `warm amber` stick figure representing "You" is the constant central character throughout all scenes. The `white` stick figures/lines represent the queues, which shift from static to dynamic. `Coral red` is consistently used to denote frustration, bad luck, or the biased memory imprint. `Signal blue` emerges in the final scene to represent the overarching system and clarity. The camera's perspective generally follows the amber figure, shifting from close-up frustration to a broader, more abstract understanding of the system.
