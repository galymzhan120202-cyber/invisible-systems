# Production Plan: Why Games Show Loading Screen Tips

**System:** User engagement and tutorial delivery during unavoidable downtime in video games.
**Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female voice (e.g., AvaNeural/JennyNeural).
**Palette:** `signal blue` (the screen, the system of tips), `warm amber` (the player, their controller, desire to play), `coral red` (frustration of waiting, naive assumption).
**Hero Element:** A stick-figure player (amber), a game controller (amber), and a game screen (blue, transforming).
