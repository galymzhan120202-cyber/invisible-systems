# Production Plan: Why Weather Forecasts Are Often Vague

**System:** Inherent unpredictability and probabilistic modeling of chaotic systems
**Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult female voice
**Palette:** `signal blue` (system/rules), `warm amber` (you/desire), `coral red` (naive/wrong model)
**Hero Element:** A cloud, representing the weather system, which transforms from simple icon to complex, diverging paths, then a probabilistic field.

| # | Time | Story beat | Stick-figure scene | Motion / camera / transition | English VO | SFX |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–10s | **YOU + NEED (part 1)**: Checking the forecast | Amber stick-figure looking at a phone with a simple white cloud icon. A question mark hovers near the cloud. | Camera on stick-figure, then zooms slightly to focus on the phone screen and the cloud icon. | Every morning, you check your weather app, hoping for a clear answer. Will it rain today, or will the sun shine all day long? | click-soft.mp3 (0.6s), focus.mp3 (1.5s) |
| 2 | 10–20s | **NEED (part 2)**: Desire for certainty | Amber stick-figure with two thought bubbles: one with a sunny picnic basket, one with an umbrella. The cloud icon from Scene 1 persists, now with an added question mark inside. | Camera pans slightly to show the stick-figure's planning. Thought bubbles appear and disappear quickly. | You need to make plans, decide on outfits, or whether to bring an umbrella. You just want a simple yes or no answer, a guarantee. | whoosh.mp3 (12.0s) |
| 3 | 20–30s | **GO**: Frustratingly vague forecast | Amber stick-figure shrugging, looking at the phone. The cloud icon is now larger, with `40%` and `Scattered` in coral red inside it, implying vagueness. | Stick-figure shrugs. Phone screen animates the vague text appearing. Camera stays on the stick-figure and phone. | But often, the forecast is frustratingly vague. "40% chance of showers," or "partly cloudy with isolated thunderstorms." It feels like they don't really know. | drop-thud.mp3 (21.5s) |
| 4 | 30–40s | **SEARCH**: Why the vagueness? | Amber stick-figure with a confused expression. Behind them, a complex weather map with swirling coral red lines emerges, still framed by the cloud outline. | Camera slowly pulls back to reveal the complex weather map. Stick-figure's expression changes from shrug to confusion. | You wonder why they can't just tell you for sure. Is the technology not advanced enough, or are meteorologists just trying to cover their bases? | focus.mp3 (30.7s), whoosh-fast.mp3 (32.5s) |
| 5 | 40–50s | **FIND**: Chaotic system revealed | The complex cloud outline breaks into multiple, diverging signal blue lines/paths, originating from a single point. The amber stick-figure watches, beginning to understand. | The coral red lines dissipate, replaced by signal blue lines that dynamically diverge from a central point, illustrating chaos. | The truth is, weather is a chaotic system. Tiny, unmeasurable differences in initial conditions rapidly amplify, leading to vastly different outcomes over time. | transform.mp3 (41.0s), whoosh.mp3 (44.5s) |
| 6 | 50–60s | **TAKE + RETURN/CHANGE**: Embracing probabilistic understanding | Amber stick-figure looks at a unified cloud shape, now rendered with subtle `signal blue` gradients and varying densities, representing a probabilistic field. The picnic basket and umbrella icons reappear, smaller, next to the stick-figure. | The diverging lines coalesce into a single, yet nuanced, probabilistic cloud. Stick-figure nods in understanding. Camera slowly zooms out. | Forecasts use many simulations to show a range of possibilities, not one definite future. The vagueness is actually an honest representation of nature's complexity. | complete-done.mp3 (51.5s), click-soft.mp3 (56.0s) |

**Continuity map:**
- **Scene 1 to 2:** The amber stick-figure and the cloud icon on the phone persist, transitioning from checking to planning.
- **Scene 2 to 3:** The stick-figure's focus shifts from planning to reacting to the phone's vague display. The cloud icon transforms to show vague text.
- **Scene 3 to 4:** The stick-figure's confusion grows as the vague cloud transforms into a complex, abstract weather map.
- **Scene 4 to 5:** The complex map and coral red lines give way to signal blue lines, dynamically diverging from a point, representing the chaotic system. The stick-figure remains, observing.
- **Scene 5 to 6:** The diverging lines merge into a single, gradient-filled cloud, signifying a probabilistic understanding. The stick-figure is now equipped with both picnic and umbrella icons, reflecting their new flexibility.
