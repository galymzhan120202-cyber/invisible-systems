# Why Coffee Shops Write Your Name on Cups

**System:** Personalization tactics to enhance customer experience and brand loyalty
**Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female voice
**Palette:** Black canvas, white line art. Accent colors: signal blue (system/connection), warm amber (you/desire), coral red (confusion/inefficiency).
**Hero Elements:** The customer (amber stick figure), the coffee cup, the coffee shop counter/barista.
