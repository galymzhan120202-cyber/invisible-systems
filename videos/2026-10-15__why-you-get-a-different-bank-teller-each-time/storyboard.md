# Production Plan: Why You Get a Different Bank Teller Each Time

**System:** Task allocation and shift management in service industries
**Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female voice (e.g., AvaNeural/JennyNeural)
**Palette:** warm amber (You/desire), coral red (naive model/confusion), signal blue (system/logic)
**Hero Element:** A bank teller counter/service desk, transforming to show underlying structure.

| # | Time | Story beat | Stick-figure scene | Motion / camera / transition | English VO | SFX |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–10s | **YOU + NEED**: Desire for personal, quick service. | A single amber stick-figure (YOU) approaches a simple white teller counter. A thought bubble above YOU shows a smiling, familiar teller icon. | Camera follows YOU approaching the counter. Slow pan across the counter. | You enter the bank, hoping for a quick transaction. You might even look for a familiar face, expecting a personal connection to make things smoother. | `whoosh-fast.mp3` (0.6s) <br> `focus.mp3` (5.0s) |
| 2 | 10–20s | **GO**: Realization of constant change. | YOU (amber) approaches the same counter. A new, different white stick-figure teller appears behind it. This repeats quickly, showing several different tellers. | Quick cuts between YOU approaching the counter and a *different* teller appearing behind it each time. YOU's expression turns slightly confused. | But next time, it’s someone new. And again after that, someone else. It truly feels like you never see the same teller twice in a row. | `whoosh.mp3` (10.5s) <br> `drop-thud.mp3` (18.0s) |
| 3 | 20–30s | **SEARCH**: Pondering the "why" – naive assumptions. | YOU (amber) stands at the counter, a large coral red question mark floats above, then morphs into a chaotic pile of individual teller icons, implying random assignment. | Camera slowly zooms in on YOU and the coral red confusion. The random pile of teller icons (coral red) shuffles chaotically. | You start to wonder why. Is the bank just randomly assigning staff? Or is there some secret system preventing you from building any real rapport? | `focus.mp3` (22.0s) <br> `click-soft.mp3` (27.0s) |
| 4 | 30–40s | **FIND**: The true mechanism revealed. | The counter transforms into a signal blue abstract diagram. It shows multiple service "slots" (blue rectangles) and white stick-figure tellers moving between them, connected by blue lines indicating tasks/skills. | The coral red elements dissolve as the counter transforms into the signal blue system diagram. Tellers (white) move purposefully within the blue structure. | Banks are complex systems balancing fluctuating demand. Tellers have different skills, shifts, and tasks beyond just the service counter. | `transform.mp3` (30.8s) <br> `complete-done.mp3` (37.0s) |
| 5 | 40–50s | **TAKE**: The trade-off: efficiency over personalization. | Two scenarios side-by-side: one shows a single long amber queue for one specific teller, while the other shows multiple, shorter queues for many available tellers (part of the blue system). | Split screen. Left side: slow movement, frustration. Right side: dynamic, efficient movement of people to available tellers. The "slow queue" idea collapses. | So instead of one slow queue for *your* specific teller, staff are distributed across all available stations, ensuring someone is always ready. | `whoosh-fast.mp3` (41.5s) <br> `stack-collapse.mp3` (48.0s) |
| 6 | 50–60s | **RETURN + CHANGE**: New understanding. | YOU (amber) approaches the teller counter again, but now subtle signal blue lines emanate from the counter, showing the invisible system at work. YOU looks understanding. | Camera follows YOU, then zooms out slightly to reveal the blue system lines. YOU's expression is one of calm understanding. | Now, approaching the counter, you don't just see random rotation. You see an optimized flow, designed to keep the entire operation moving efficiently. | `focus.mp3` (51.0s) <br> `complete-done.mp3` (57.0s) |

**Continuity map:**
- **Scene 1-2:** The amber 'YOU' stick-figure and the basic teller counter persist, but the specific teller changes.
- **Scene 2-3:** The amber 'YOU' and counter persist, transitioning from observation to internal questioning.
- **Scene 3-4:** The counter transforms from a simple object into an abstract diagram of the underlying system.
- **Scene 4-5:** The abstract system diagram persists, evolving to illustrate the practical outcome (queue comparison).
- **Scene 5-6:** The amber 'YOU' stick-figure and teller counter return, now imbued with the visible understanding of the invisible system.
