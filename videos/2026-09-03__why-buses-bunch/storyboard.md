# Why buses bunch up in twos and threes

*   **System:** Positive feedback loop in public transport, where delays self-reinforce and cause bunching.
*   **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female voice (e.g., AvaNeural).
*   **Accent Palette:** `signal blue` (the system, the bus flow), `warm amber` (YOU, the passengers), `coral red` (frustration, naive assumption).
*   **Hero Element:** A simple bus shape (rectangle with wheels) and stick figures representing passengers.
