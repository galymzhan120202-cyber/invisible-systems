# Why Free Refills Make Business Sense

*   **System:** Customer retention and increased consumption through perceived value.
*   **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult female voice (e.g., AvaNeural or JennyNeural).
*   **Palette:** Warm amber (customer desire/value), coral red (naive model/perceived loss), signal blue (the system/strategy).
*   **Hero Element:** A simple drink cup, which transforms to represent different aspects of the system.

**Continuity map:**
*   **Scene 1 -> Scene 2:** The stick figure and the initial cup are consistent. The environment shifts from ordering counter to the main dining area with the refill sign.
*   **Scene 2 -> Scene 3:** The stick figure and the cup remain, but the cup's color changes to reflect the internal thought process. The sign is still present in the background.
*   **Scene 3 -> Scene 4:** The stick figure is central. The cup transforms from its coral red state into the signal blue funnel, representing the shift from naive assumption to systemic understanding.
*   **Scene 4 -> Scene 5:** The stick figure and the signal blue funnel/magnet are persistent, with the addition of the amber snack to show the consequence.
*   **Scene 5 -> Scene 6:** The stick figure returns to the refill environment, now holding a standard cup, but the previously revealed blue system is subtly integrated into the refill machine itself, signifying understanding.
