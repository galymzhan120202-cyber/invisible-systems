*   **System:** Ad retargeting saturation and diminishing returns
*   **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female voice (e.g., AvaNeural)
*   **Palette:** signal blue (system logic/optimization), warm amber (viewer/desired product), coral red (naive expectation/false model)
*   **Hero Element:** Stick figure (YOU) interacting with abstract "product" shapes and 'ad' rectangles, all within a simplified digital environment grid.
