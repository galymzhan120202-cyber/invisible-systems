*   **Narrator:** bright, energetic adult female voice
*   **Palette:** signal blue (system/rule), warm amber (you/desire), coral red (naive/wrong model)
*   **Hero Element:** A row of three stylized airplane seats (window, middle, aisle) that persists and transforms.
