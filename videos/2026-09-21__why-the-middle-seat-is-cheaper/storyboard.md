# Why the Middle Seat is Cheaper on Flights

**System:** Airline pricing based on perceived value and demand
**Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female voice (e.g., AvaNeural or JennyNeural)
**Palette:** Black canvas, white line art. Accent colors: **signal blue** (the system/rules), **warm amber** (the traveler/their desires), **coral red** (discomfort/naive assumption).
**Hero Elements:** An **amber** stick figure traveler, a **white** airplane cabin layout with seats.

**Continuity map:**
*   **Scene 1 to 2:** From an abstract booking screen to the concrete experience inside the plane.
*   **Scene 2 to 3:** Focus shifts from the act of booking to the immediate, physical discomfort of the middle seat.
*   **Scene 3 to 4:** The specific discomfort transforms into a generalized conceptual diagram of value and pricing.
*   **Scene 4 to 5:** The conceptual diagram is populated by figures representing demand, illustrating how the segmentation works in practice.
*   **Scene 5 to 6:** The conceptual understanding is brought back to the personal experience, but with a new perspective.
