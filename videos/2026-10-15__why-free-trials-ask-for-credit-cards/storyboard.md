# Why Free Trials Ask for Your Credit Card

- **System:** Friction in cancellation and automated renewal incentives
- **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female voice (e.g., AvaNeural)
- **Palette:** Dark theme (black canvas, white line art), warm amber (YOU), coral red (friction/naive model), signal blue (system/rule revealed)
- **Hero Element:** A stick figure (YOU), a "Service Box" (representing the trial/service), and a "Credit Card" icon.
