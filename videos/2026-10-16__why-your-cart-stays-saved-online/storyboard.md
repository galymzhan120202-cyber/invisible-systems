# Production Plan: Why Your Online Cart Stays Saved

**System:** Persistence mechanisms and abandoned cart recovery strategies
**Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female voice (e.g., `en-US-AvaNeural`)
**Accent Palette:** `signal blue` (system/server), `warm amber` (user/desire), `coral red` (naive assumption)
**Hero Element:** A stick figure representing "YOU", and a shopping cart icon.

**Continuity map:**
- **Scene 1 to 2:** The `warm amber` stick figure (YOU) and the computer screen persist. The cart transitions from disappearing to reappearing full.
- **Scene 2 to 3:** The `warm amber` stick figure (YOU) and the full cart persist. A thought bubble is introduced.
- **Scene 3 to 4:** The `warm amber` stick figure (YOU) and the full cart persist. The thought bubble is replaced by the `signal blue` server and connection lines.
- **Scene 4 to 5:** The `warm amber` stick figure (YOU), full cart, and `signal blue` server all persist, with the addition of the `signal blue` "nudge" arrow.
- **Scene 5 to 6:** The `warm amber` stick figure (YOU) and the full cart persist, now with YOU's expression changed, and the `signal blue` system elements remaining as context.
