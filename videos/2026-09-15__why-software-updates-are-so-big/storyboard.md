# Production Plan: Why Software Updates Are So Huge

*   **System:** Cumulative feature addition and backward compatibility constraints in software architecture.
*   **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female (e.g., AvaNeural).
*   **Palette:** Signal blue (system/structure), warm amber (user desire/focus), coral red (naive expectation/friction).
*   **Hero Element:** A "Software Stack" represented as a vertical series of distinct blocks/layers.

| # | Time | Story beat | Stick-figure scene | Motion / camera / transition | English VO | SFX |
|---|------|------------|--------------------|------------------------------|------------|-----|
| 1 | 0–10s | **YOU + NEED**: User's frustration with large updates for small fixes. | A warm amber stick-figure taps on a phone icon. A large, coral red download bar fills slowly. | Camera is static, focused on the phone and download. The download bar visualizes the 'huge' aspect. | Every time you hit 'update' on your phone or computer, it's gigabytes. You just wanted a tiny bug fixed, not a whole new operating system! | `whoosh-fast.mp3` @ 1.2s (update starts), `drop-thud.mp3` @ 5.1s (frustration with size) |
| 2 | 10–20s | **GO**: The naive expectation of a tiny patch. | The stick-figure points at a tiny, specific coral red "bug" icon. An imagined, tiny coral red patch block floats above. | Focus shifts to the tiny bug. The patch block appears with a simple `whoosh`. | You imagine a small patch, a single line of code changed, sent quickly to your device. Why does it feel like a complete re-download? | `focus.mp3` @ 10.8s (on bug), `whoosh.mp3` @ 14.7s (imagined patch arrives) |
| 3 | 20–30s | **SEARCH**: The reality of software complexity as a stack. | A signal blue "Software Stack" appears: multiple distinct horizontal blocks layered vertically. **Continuity map:**

*   Scene 3, 4, 5, 6: The signal blue "Software Stack" as the primary persistent element, transforming and growing.
*   Scene 5 and 6: The expanded signal blue Software Stack.
*   Scene 6: The warm amber stick-figure (callback from S1 and S2).
