**Continuity map:**
*   **Scene 1 → Scene 2:** The initial white headlines are replaced by and overwhelmed with coral red, jagged headlines. The amber stick figure persists.
*   **Scene 2 → Scene 3:** Focus shifts from many headlines to one large coral red headline, with the amber stick figure still present, expressing frustration.
*   **Scene 3 → Scene 4:** The coral red headlines (from Scene 2's general form) are now shown interacting with the signal blue algorithm, replacing the singular 'naive' headline. The amber stick figure's initial assumption is replaced by the system.
*   **Scene 4 → Scene 5:** The signal blue algorithm persists, but its output changes to degraded coral red forms. The amber stick figure reappears, reacting to the outcome.
*   **Scene 5 → Scene 6:** The degraded coral red forms recede slightly, and the amber stick figure returns to the scrolling action from Scene 1, but with newfound understanding (blue glow).
