**Continuity map:**
The amber stick figure is the persistent protagonist. The "screen" or "website" element evolves from a generic browsing interface to a social media feed, then to abstract boxes representing websites. The headphone icon consistently represents the product of interest. The small blue "pixel" icon is introduced in Scene 4 and remains visible on the stick figure throughout the remaining scenes.
