# Production Plan: Why Supermarkets Play Soothing Music

**System:** Auditory cues influencing customer mood and dwell time
**Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female voice (en-US-AvaNeural)
**Palette:** `signal blue` (music/system), `warm amber` (shopper/desire), `coral red` (naive thought/slowed pace)
**Hero Element:** A stick figure pushing a shopping cart, with abstract sound waves.

| # | Time | Story beat | Stick-figure scene | Motion / camera / transition | English VO | SFX |
|---|------|------------|--------------------|------------------------------|------------|-----|
| 1 | 0–10s | **YOU + NEED**: Entering, ready to shop. | A stick figure (warm amber) pushes a shopping cart (white) into a simple supermarket entrance (white arch). Simple shelves (white lines) are visible inside. | Camera follows the stick figure entering from left to right. Cart moves at a normal pace. | You step into the supermarket, ready to grab your groceries. You just want to get your list done and maybe find a good deal. | `whoosh.mp3`, `click-soft.mp3` |
| 2 | 10–20s | **GO**: Notice the music, feel calm. | Stick figure (amber) and cart (white) move slowly past shelves. Gentle, wavy `signal blue` lines (music) appear around them, filling the background. | Camera slowly pans right with the stick figure. The music waves gently pulse. | A gentle melody floats through the aisles. You might barely notice it, but it makes the environment feel calm and inviting. | `whoosh.mp3`, `focus.mp3` |
| 3 | 20–30s | **SEARCH**: Questioning the purpose. | Stick figure (amber) stops, hand to chin in a thinking pose. A large, stylized question mark (white) appears above their head. The music waves (signal blue) continue gently. A small `coral red` thought bubble briefly appears. | Camera zooms slightly on the stick figure. The thought bubble appears and fades quickly. | Is it just background noise, or is there a clever plan behind these tunes? You might think it's simply to make your shopping experience more pleasant. | `focus.mp3`, `transform.mp3` |
| 4 | 30–40s | **FIND**: The mechanism of slowing down. | Stick figure (amber) and cart (white) are now moving noticeably slower. The `signal blue` music waves are thicker, visibly 'pushing' against the cart, slowing its progress. A simple clock icon (white, no digits) has its hands visibly slowing their rotation. | Camera slowly tracks the very slow movement. The blue waves seem to exert a force. Clock hands slow down. | But the real genius is how that slow tempo subtly slows *you* down. Your pace matches the rhythm, encouraging you to linger longer in each aisle. | `whoosh.mp3`, `transform.mp3` |
| 5 | 40–50s | **TAKE**: The cost — impulse buys. | The shopping cart (white) is now overflowing with various small, simple item shapes (white and `warm amber`). The stick figure (amber) gestures towards an item that drops in. A wallet icon (white) with coins (white) shrinks slightly. | Items quickly fill the cart. An `amber` item drops in. The wallet icon visually depletes. | More time spent browsing means more chances to spot things not on your list. That soothing music actually nudges you towards impulse buys, costing you extra time and money. | `click-soft.mp3`, `drop-thud.mp3` |
| 6 | 50–60s | **RETURN + CHANGE**: Aware of the system. | Stick figure (amber) pushes the cart (white, still full but not overflowing) out of the supermarket exit. The `signal blue` music waves are still present, but the stick figure briefly looks directly at the camera with a knowing nod. | Camera pulls back slightly as the stick figure exits. The nod is a subtle, conscious movement. | Next time you’re pushing your cart, listen closely. You’ll hear the system at play, gently guiding your journey, now with open eyes. | `complete-done.mp3`, `whoosh-fast.mp3` |

**Continuity map:**
- Scene 1 & 2: Stick figure and shopping cart, supermarket interior.
- Scene 2 & 3: Stick figure and cart, music waves.
- Scene 3 & 4: Stick figure and cart, music waves, emphasis on pacing.
- Scene 4 & 5: Stick figure and cart, music waves, now with more items.
- Scene 5 & 6: Stick figure and cart, music waves, exiting the store.
