# Why Your Phone Suggests the Next Word

*   **System:** Predictive text algorithms learning user patterns
*   **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult female (e.g., AvaNeural)
*   **Palette:** Dark canvas, white line art. Accent colors: signal blue (system/patterns), warm amber (user's intent/self), coral red (naive/wrong model).
*   **Hero Element:** A stick figure's warm amber hand typing on a phone screen, which displays abstract word suggestions.

**Continuity map:**
The warm amber stick figure hand and the phone are persistent elements throughout all scenes. The phone screen's content evolves from simple typing, to generic suggestions, to showing internal learning patterns, and finally to reflecting the user's specific linguistic identity. Abstract bubble shapes represent word suggestions, transforming from grey to signal blue as the system is revealed.
