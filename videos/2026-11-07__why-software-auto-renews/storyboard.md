# Why Software Subscriptions Auto-Renew

- **System:** Subscription models designed to capture inertia and reduce churn.
- **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female voice.
- **Palette:** Black canvas, white line art. Accent colors: `warm amber` (user/desire), `signal blue` (software/system), `coral red` (naive thought/friction).
- **Hero Element:** An `amber` stick figure and a `signal blue` generic 'software box' icon that represents the subscription service.

**Continuity map:**
- **Scene 1 to 2:** Transition from focused user to busy user, software box fades, clock appears.
- **Scene 2 to 3:** Charge icon persists, user's focus shifts to internal thought.
- **Scene 3 to 4:** User's confusion fades, software box reappears and becomes transparent to reveal mechanism.
- **Scene 4 to 5:** Mechanism continues, now showing its effect (money flow).
- **Scene 5 to 6:** Money flow stops, user is back at desk, software box is now understood, new confident action.
