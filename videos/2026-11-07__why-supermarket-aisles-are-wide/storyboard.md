# Why Supermarket Aisles Are So Wide

*   **System:** Layout design to encourage browsing and impulse purchases by slowing down shoppers.
*   **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female voice (e.g., `en-US-AvaNeural`).
*   **Palette:** `signal blue` (the system/guidance), `warm amber` (the shopper/their desires), `coral red` (naive assumptions/unplanned purchases).
*   **Hero Element:** An `amber` stick-figure shopper with a shopping cart, and the changing geometry of the supermarket aisle itself.
