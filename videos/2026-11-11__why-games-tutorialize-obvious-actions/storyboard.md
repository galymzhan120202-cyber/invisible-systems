# Why Games Tutorialize Obvious Actions

*   **System:** Onboarding and ensuring baseline engagement for all players
*   **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult female voice
*   **Palette:** Dark theme (black canvas, white line art), warm amber (player), coral red (naive model), signal blue (system/mechanism)
*   **Hero Element:** A stick-figure player (warm amber) interacting with a game screen/interface.
