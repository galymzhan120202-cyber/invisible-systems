# Why Supermarket Music Is So Slow

*   **System:** Pacing and impulse buying
*   **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult female voice (en-US-AvaNeural or en-US-JennyNeural)
*   **Palette:** Dark theme (black canvas, white line art). Accent colors: **signal blue** (system/music's effect), **warm amber** (your desires/impulse buys), **coral red** (naive attempt to rush).
*   **Hero Element:** A stick-figure pushing a shopping cart.
