# Why Apps Ask for All Permissions at Once

*   **System:** Bundling of permissions to reduce user friction on individual approvals
*   **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult female voice (e.g., AvaNeural, JennyNeural)
*   **Palette:** Signal blue (system/rule), Warm amber (user's desire/action), Coral red (naive assumption/friction)
*   **Hero Element:** The Phone Screen, which displays app interactions and transforms to show permission requests and their resolution.
