# Production Plan: Why Doors Open Differently

**System:** Hardware constraints and universal design principles
**Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female voice (e.g., AvaNeural)
**Palette:** Signal blue (system/rules), Warm amber (you/desire), Coral red (naive model/friction)
**Hero Element:** A simple rectangular door frame and slab, which transforms to show its context and mechanics.

**Continuity map:**
The `stick-figure character` (amber) is present in scenes 1, 2, 3, and 6, showing their journey from confusion to understanding. The `door` itself is the persistent hero element, transforming from a generic obstacle (scenes 1-3) to a transparent system (scene 4), then illustrating consequences (scene 5), and finally becoming a clearly understood object (scene 6). The background shifts between a generic building entrance and abstract representations of space/crowds.
