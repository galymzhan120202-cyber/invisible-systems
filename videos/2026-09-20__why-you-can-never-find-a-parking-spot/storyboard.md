*   **System:** Supply-demand mismatch and search friction in urban planning.
*   **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female (en-US-AvaNeural).
*   **Palette:** Dark theme (black canvas, white lines). Accent colors: `warm amber` (YOU / car / desire), `signal blue` (the system / mechanism), `coral red` (frustration / scarcity / naive model).
*   **Hero Element:** An `amber` stick-figure car.

*   **Scene 3-4:** The physical parking lot fades, and the abstract `signal blue` system diagram appears, with the `amber` car's path overlaid.

*   **Scene 5-6:** The `amber` car finally parks, and the visual focus shifts to its newfound understanding of the system.
