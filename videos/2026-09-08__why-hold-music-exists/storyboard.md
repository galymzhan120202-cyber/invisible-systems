# Why 'please hold' music exists

*   **System:** Silence reads as a dropped call / perceived wait time
*   **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female (en-US-AvaNeural)
*   **Accent Palette:** Warm amber (user/desire), Coral red (naive model/failure), Signal blue (system/solution)
*   **Hero Element:** Stick figure holding a phone, and a "sound wave" line that transforms.
