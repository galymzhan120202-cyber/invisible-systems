# Why the Popcorn Costs More Than the Movie Ticket

*   **System:** Two-part pricing / where the profit actually sits
*   **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult female voice
*   **Palette:** Signal Blue (system/rules), Warm Amber (you/wants), Coral Red (naive model/friction)
*   **Hero Element:** A "Value Icon" (a simple coin/stack of money) that represents the cost and transforms to show profit distribution.

**Continuity map:**
*   **Stick figure:** Persists throughout, in a cinema setting.
*   **Cinema environment:** Simple background remains consistent, transitioning between lobby/stand and seating area.
*   **Value Icon:** Starts as a small ticket, transforms into a large popcorn bucket, then a bar chart, then a funding source, and finally a re-contextualized popcorn bucket.
*   **Color Use:** `Warm amber` for the viewer's desires/objects (ticket, popcorn), `coral red` for confusion/misconception, `signal blue` for the revealed system and its components.
