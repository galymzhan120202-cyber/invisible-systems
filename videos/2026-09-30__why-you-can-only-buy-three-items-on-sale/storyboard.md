## Production Plan: "Why You Can Only Buy Three Items on Sale"

**System:** Purchase limits as a price discrimination and inventory management tool
**Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female voice, natural American English.
**Palette:** Signal Blue (the system/rule), Warm Amber (the customer's desire/gain), Coral Red (the naive attempt/misunderstanding).
**Hero Element:** A stick figure representing "You" starts with a shopping cart. The cart transforms scene by scene: first empty, then overflowing with coral red items, then getting a coral red 'X' or barrier, then becoming a smaller, more controlled amber cart, and finally just a simple amber cart again.

**Continuity map:**
*   **Scene 1 to 2:** Zoomed-in focus on the 'LIMIT 3' icon transitions to a fast-paced motion as the figure starts shopping.
*   **Scene 2 to 3:** The rapidly filling cart transitions to an overflowing cart hitting a barrier, signifying the *search* for a way around the limit.
*   **Scene 3 to 4:** The confusion of hitting the barrier transitions to the *finding* of the underlying mechanism via visual explanation.
*   **Scene 4 to 5:** The explanation of the system transitions to the practical *taking* of the deal under its rules.
*   **Scene 5 to 6:** The satisfied shopper with their regulated purchase transitions to a final understanding of the 'LIMIT 3' icon.
