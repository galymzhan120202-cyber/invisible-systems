# Why Airlines Charge for Checked Bags

*   **System:** Unbundling services to create tiered pricing and perceived value.
*   **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female (e.g., AvaNeural).
*   **Palette:** Dark theme. `warm amber` for the traveler/their desires, `coral red` for frustration/naive assumptions, `signal blue` for the revealed system/structure.
*   **Hero Element:** An `amber` rectangular travel bag, representing the traveler's luggage and perceived 'value'.
