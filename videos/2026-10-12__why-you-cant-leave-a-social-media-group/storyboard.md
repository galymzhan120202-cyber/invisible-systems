# Production Plan: Why You Can't Leave Some Social Media Groups

**System:** Platform design discouraging departure via nudges and friction
**Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female voice (en-US-AvaNeural)
**Accent Palette:** Warm Amber (You/Desire), Coral Red (Friction/Naive Attempt), Signal Blue (System/Mechanism)
**Hero Elements:** An amber stick figure (YOU), a social media group represented by dots/shapes, and an "exit" icon.

**Continuity map:**
- The **amber stick figure** remains the persistent protagonist, experiencing the system.
- The **social media group** starts as loose, noisy grey dots (Scene 1), transforms into a "Leave Group" button (Scene 2), then a deceptive coral red pop-up (Scene 3), then a revealed signal blue system structure (Scene 4), an enclosing cage of clutter (Scene 5), and finally a distant, understood backdrop (Scene 6).
- The **act of leaving** transitions from a simple desire to a frustrated attempt, then to an insight into deliberate design, and finally to a recognition of persistent entrapment.
