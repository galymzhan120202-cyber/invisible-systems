# Production Plan: Why Doctors Use Appointment Slots

**System:** Scheduling to balance physician time and patient flow
**Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult female voice (e.g., AvaNeural or JennyNeural)
**Palette:** `signal blue` (system/structure), `warm amber` (patient/desire), `coral red` (naive model/frustration)
**Hero Element:** A clock/calendar icon that transforms into time blocks and a scheduling grid.

| # | Time | Story beat | Stick-figure scene | Motion / camera / transition | English VO | SFX |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–10s | **YOU + NEED** | Amber stick figure with a small coral red 'X' on its head (minor ailment). A white clock icon spins rapidly beside it. | Camera fixed on amber stick figure. Small 'X' appears. Clock icon appears and spins faster. | You've got a minor health issue, nothing urgent. You just want it fixed quickly, hoping for a simple walk-in or a fast slot today. | `drop-thud.mp3`<br>`whoosh-fast.mp3` |
| 2 | 10–20s | **GO** | Amber stick figure holds a white phone icon. The clock icon from Scene 1 morphs into a white calendar grid. Today's date has a coral red 'X', and a white bubble reads "3 DAYS" next to a future white slot. | Camera pans slightly to follow the stick figure. Clock transforms into grid. 'X' and bubble appear. | But calling the clinic, you hit the wall: "The next available appointment is in three days." Your heart sinks. Why can't you just show up? | `click-soft.mp3`<br>`stack-collapse.mp3` |
| 3 | 20–30s | **SEARCH** | Amber stick figure looks into an empty white waiting room (chairs, desk). Behind the desk, a small white stick figure doctor sips from a coral red coffee cup, with a coral red thought bubble showing leisure. | Camera pans into the waiting room. Doctor and thought bubble appear. | You imagine an empty waiting room, or doctors sipping coffee, ignoring patients. It feels inefficient, like the system wants to make things harder for you. | `focus.mp3`<br>`drop-thud.mp3` |
| 4 | 30–40s | **FIND** | The waiting room dissolves. Large, empty white 'time blocks' appear. A white stick figure doctor appears, and the blocks rapidly fill with small white icons (stethoscope, computer). The blocks then transform into a clean, structured signal blue grid. | Scene elements dissolve. Blocks appear, fill with icons, then transform into blue grid. | The truth is, a doctor's time is a finite resource. Appointment slots are a clever system, designed in signal blue, to allocate that scarcity and prevent overwhelming surges. | `focus.mp3`<br>`transform.mp3` |
| 5 | 40–50s | **TAKE** | The signal blue grid from Scene 4 is present. Many amber stick figures try to push into the grid, causing coral red cracks and distortions. A single amber stick figure (YOU) watches patiently as the others form a neat queue. | Grid warps and cracks as figures push. Grid re-stabilizes and figures form a queue. | Without them, everyone would rush in at once, creating endless, chaotic queues and burnt-out doctors. You pay with your wait, ensuring the system stays fair. | `stack-collapse.mp3`<br>`complete-done.mp3` |
| 6 | 50–60s | **RETURN + CHANGE** | The signal blue calendar grid from Scene 2 reappears. The future appointment slot glows warm amber. The amber stick figure (YOU) looks at it, then nods knowingly as a white thought bubble with the structured blue grid appears above its head. | Calendar grid reappears, slot glows. Stick figure nods, thought bubble appears. | Now, when you book that future slot, you see past the frustration. You understand the invisible system keeping healthcare from utter collapse. | `focus.mp3`<br>`complete-done.mp3` |

**Continuity map:**
- **Scene 1 to 2:** The patient (amber stick figure) and the concept of time (clock/calendar) persist and evolve.
- **Scene 2 to 3:** The patient (amber stick figure) remains, transitioning from the call to imagining the clinic.
- **Scene 3 to 4:** The concept of the doctor's time and its management connects the imagined inefficiency to the real system.
- **Scene 4 to 5:** The signal blue scheduling grid, representing the system, persists and is tested by patient demand. The patient (YOU) watches.
- **Scene 5 to 6:** The signal blue scheduling grid persists, now seen through the patient's newfound understanding. The patient (YOU) is present and transformed by knowledge.
