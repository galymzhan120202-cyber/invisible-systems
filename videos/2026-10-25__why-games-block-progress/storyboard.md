*   **System:** Intentional pacing and difficulty curves to maximize engagement
*   **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult female voice (en-US-AvaNeural)
*   **Palette:** `warm amber` (player), `coral red` (frustration/naive model), `signal blue` (system/mechanism)
*   **Hero Element:** A `warm amber` stick-figure player. A 'progress bar' that evolves into a 'difficulty gate' and then into 'system gears'.
