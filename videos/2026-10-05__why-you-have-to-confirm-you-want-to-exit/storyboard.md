**Continuity map:**
The warm amber stick figure is the constant protagonist, their emotional state and understanding evolving. The white rectangular "screen" interface is present throughout, changing its content and overlaid elements to reflect the narrative. The 'X' button symbol is a recurring visual anchor, first as a target, then as part of the frustrating prompt, then as an understood part of the system. The camera remains mostly static, focusing on the stick figure and screen interaction.
