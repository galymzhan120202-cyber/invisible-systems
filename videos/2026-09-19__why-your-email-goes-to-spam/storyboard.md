# Production Plan: Why Your Email Goes to Spam

## Video Title
Why Your Email Goes to Spam

## System
Reputational scoring and filtering algorithms

## Narrator
Bright, energetic adult female voice (e.g., AvaNeural or JennyNeural).

## Palette
**Warm amber:** The sender (YOU), their email.
**Coral red:** The naive, incorrect understanding of spam filtering.
**Signal blue:** The actual, complex reputational scoring system.

## Hero Element
An "email" (represented as a stylized envelope or data packet) and a "filter/gate" that processes it.

## Continuity map
- **Scene 1-2:** The amber envelope travels from the stick figure's computer towards an inbox, only to be stopped by the 'FILTER' wall.
- **Scene 2-3:** The 'FILTER' wall persists as the obstacle, transitioning from generic gray to a coral red "keyword scanner" representation.
- **Scene 3-4:** The 'FILTER' wall transforms from a simple coral red keyword scanner into a complex signal blue network, revealing the true mechanism.
- **Scene 4-5:** The signal blue network persists, now demonstrating how reputation (amber dots) affects its internal state.
- **Scene 5-6:** The signal blue network remains, acting as the gatekeeper, now allowing the amber email to pass through due to an improved (implied) reputation. The stick figure and email are consistent throughout.
