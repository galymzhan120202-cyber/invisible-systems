## Why We Feel Pressured to Tip

**System:** Social norms and wage structures that shift labor costs to consumers
**Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult female (en-US-AvaNeural)
**Palette:** Black canvas, white line art. Accent colors: **signal blue** (system/rule), **warm amber** (customer/desire), **coral red** (naive model).
**Hero Element:** A rectangular "check/bill" and the stick-figure customer.
