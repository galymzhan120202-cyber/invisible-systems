# Why You Don't See Ads For Things You Search

**System:** Ad algorithms optimizing for future purchases over immediate searches

**Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult female voice (e.g., AvaNeural or JennyNeural)

**Palette:**
- `signal blue`: algorithm's logic, prediction
- `warm amber`: viewer's current desire, "YOU"
- `coral red`: irrelevant ads, naive expectation

**Hero Element:**
A stick figure (YOU, in warm amber) interacting with a rectangular "search bar" which transforms into a "screen", and then into a "prediction engine". Ads appear as simple rectangular blocks.
