# Why supermarket milk is at the very back

**System:** Store layout as a forced path past high-margin goods
**Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult female (e.g., AvaNeural)
**Palette:** Dark theme (black canvas, white line art). Accent colors: warm amber (shopper/desire), coral red (naive idea/impulse buys), signal blue (system/path).
**Hero Element:** An amber stick-figure shopper with a shopping cart, and a milk carton icon.
