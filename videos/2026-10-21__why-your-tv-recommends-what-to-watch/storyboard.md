# Why Your TV Recommends What to Watch

*   **System:** Algorithmic content curation based on viewing history and user patterns.
*   **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female voice.
*   **Palette:** Warm amber (viewer's desires/choices), signal blue (the algorithm/system), coral red (naive assumptions).
*   **Hero Element:** A stick figure viewer, and the evolving representation of the TV screen / recommendation system as a data flow/network.

**Continuity map:**
*   **Scene 1 -> 2:** The stick figure remains seated, the remote in hand. The TV screen transitions from generic content to specific recommendations.
*   **Scene 2 -> 3:** The stick figure remains in place, their expression changing. The TV screen's recommendations fade, making way for the naive "brain" icon.
*   **Scene 3 -> 4:** The stick figure maintains position. The coral red "brain" shatters, and the signal blue network emerges from the TV's location, expanding to connect with the viewer.
*   **Scene 4 -> 5:** The stick figure and TV remain. The signal blue network expands further to form an enclosing bubble, with the content on the TV shifting to show homogenous recommendations.
*   **Scene 5 -> 6:** The stick figure and TV are static. The signal blue bubble recedes, and a 'consciousness' icon appears above the stick figure, with subtle new path options on the TV.
