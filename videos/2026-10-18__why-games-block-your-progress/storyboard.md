# Why Games Block Your Progress

**System:** Difficulty scaling and engagement loops
**Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult female (e.g., AvaNeural)
**Palette:** warm amber (player/desire), signal blue (system/progress), coral red (obstacles/failure)
**Hero Element:** Stick figure player (amber) and a progress bar (blue) that represents their goal.
