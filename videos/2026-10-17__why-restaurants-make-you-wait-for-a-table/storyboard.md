**Continuity map:**
*   **Restaurant outline:** Persists and forms the consistent setting.
*   **Customer stick figure (YOU):** The amber stick figure is the main character, moving from outside, to waiting, to seated.
*   **Tables:** The tables (square/circle shapes) are present throughout, changing outline color (blue, coral red, signal blue) to reflect their state and role in the system.
*   **Queue:** The wait line of amber stick figures appears and visually changes length.
*   **Color scheme:** Coral red highlights the initial, naive understanding; signal blue reveals the underlying system and its mechanics; warm amber represents the customer's desire and the perceived value.
