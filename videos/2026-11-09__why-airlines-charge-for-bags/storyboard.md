# Why Airlines Charge for Checked Bags

- **System:** Unbundling services to offer lower base fares and increase ancillary revenue
- **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female (e.g., AvaNeural)
- **Palette:** Black canvas, white line art. Accent colors: warm amber (you/desire), coral red (naive model/friction), signal blue (system/mechanism).
- **Hero Element:** A luggage bag, which transforms to represent cost, unbundling, and understanding.

**Continuity map:**
- **Scene 1 to 2:** The warm amber stick figure and suitcase persist, moving from anticipation to the checkout process.
- **Scene 2 to 3:** The stick figure and suitcase remain, but the focus shifts from a specific fee to general frustration and questioning.
- **Scene 3 to 4:** The suitcase from the previous scene visually breaks apart to illustrate the concept of "unbundling."
- **Scene 4 to 5:** The unbundled components from Scene 4 lead into the concept of ancillary revenue, with the components feeding into a revenue stream.
- **Scene 5 to 6:** The stick figure and suitcase return, now with a new understanding, linking back to the initial flight desire.
