# Why Weather Forecasts Are Often Vague

**System:** Communicating uncertainty and probabilities
**Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female voice (e.g., AvaNeural or JennyNeural)
**Palette:**
  - **warm amber:** YOU (the stick figure's desires/actions)
  - **coral red:** naive/wrong model, frustration, explicit uncertainty
  - **signal blue:** the actual system, probabilistic models
**Hero Element:** A stick figure (YOU), a simple cloud icon, and abstract lines representing forecasts/paths.

**Continuity map:**
The stick figure (YOU) is the central character, evolving from seeking certainty to frustrated to finally understanding. The "weather forecast" is a persistent element, visually transforming from a simple icon to vague text, then to abstract diverging paths, and finally to a probabilistic cloud, reflecting the core mechanism of communicating uncertainty. The phone screen acts as a consistent interface for the forecast in the initial scenes.
