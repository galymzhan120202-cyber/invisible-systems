# Why News Headlines Are So Clickbaity

- **System:** Attention economy metrics that prioritize clicks over content
- **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult female (e.g., AvaNeural)
- **Palette:** `signal blue` (system/clicks), `warm amber` (viewer/information), `coral red` (frustration/misleading content)
- **Hero Element:** A simple rectangle representing a news headline/feed, which transforms to reflect its content and the underlying system.
