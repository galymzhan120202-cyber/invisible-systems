# Why Popcorn Costs More Than The Movie Ticket

*   **System:** Ancillary revenue streams and price discrimination in entertainment.
*   **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female voice.
*   **Palette:** Warm amber (You/desire), Coral red (naive model/shock), Signal blue (the system/profit).
*   **Hero Element:** The stick figure (amber) and the popcorn (evolving from icon to financial symbol).
