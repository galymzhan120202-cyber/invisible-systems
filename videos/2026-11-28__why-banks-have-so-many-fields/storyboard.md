# Why Bank Forms Ask for So Much Info

*   **System:** Regulatory compliance and risk mitigation through data collection
*   **Narrator:** Bright, energetic adult-female voice (e.g., AvaNeural)
*   **Palette:** `signal blue` (system/rules/protection), `warm amber` (user/desire/understanding), `coral red` (frustration/naive assumptions/threats)
*   **Hero Element:** A "form" or "document" that transforms. Starts as a blank sheet, fills with fields, becomes a shield, then a completed (but understood) document. A stick figure interacts with it.
